EditKit

Simple media tools for Android. Your files never leave your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Tools

  • Pitch & Speed: change the speed and pitch of audio, with a live preview.
  • Audio Converter: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS and OGG.
  • Video → Audio: take the sound out of a video.
  • Image Converter: JPG, PNG and WEBP, with resizing.
  • Audio Cutter (Pro): remove parts of a song, and the rest joins smoothly.

Everything runs on your phone. No account, no uploads.
